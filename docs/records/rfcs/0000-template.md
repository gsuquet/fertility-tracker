## Summary

Write one paragraph that tells what the proposal changes.

## Motivation

Tell why the change is necessary. Give the problems of the current state and the result that the change must give.

## Guide-level explanation

Tell how a user or a contributor sees the change. Use examples. Write this section as if the change is done.

## Reference-level explanation

Give the full rules of the change. Give the new files, the new rules, a static HTML mockup of new screens and the changes to the current reference pages. A contributor must be able to do the work from this section.

## Drawbacks

Give the costs and the risks of the change.

## Rationale and alternatives

Tell why this design is the best. Give the other designs and why the RFC does not select them. Tell what occurs if the project does not make the change.

## Prior art

Give similar solutions in other projects and standards. Tell what this RFC takes from them.

## Unresolved questions

Give the questions that the discussion of the RFC must answer before the project accepts it.

## Future possibilities

Give the work that can come after this change. This section does not decide that work.
