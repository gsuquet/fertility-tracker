# Delete all data

Use this procedure to remove all your observations from this browser. For example, do this after the tutorial or before you give the device to a different person.

> **WARNING:** After you click **Delete the copy**, you cannot undo this procedure. Make a backup first if you want to keep your data. Read [Back up and restore your data](back-up-and-restore-your-data.md).

## Procedure

1. Click the gear (**Settings**) in the header.
2. In **Data**, click **Delete all data**. The **Delete all chart data?** dialog opens.
3. Click **Delete all data**, or an action of [On a synced device](#on-a-synced-device). The undo toast shows.
4. To remove the observations from the browser fully, click **Delete the copy**.

## On a synced device

On an owner device that syncs, the dialog has two actions in place of **Delete all data**.

| Condition                                            | Result                                                                                                             |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| You give the device to a different person            | Click **Delete on this device only**. The device leaves the vault. Your other devices keep the chart.              |
| You want to delete the chart on each of your devices | Click **Delete on all devices**. The device stays in the vault, and the next sync deletes the days on each device. |
| **Else**                                             | Click **Cancel**. The data does not change.                                                                        |

> **CAUTION:** **Delete on all devices** keeps the device in the vault. Do not give this device to a different person after it. That person gets your chart at the next sync.

## Undo the delete

1. In the undo toast, click **Undo**.

The application shows your observations again.

## Result

The application has no observations. It also deletes the copy of unreadable data. After you click **Delete the copy**, it keeps no copy of your observations. The theme, the language and the welcome guide settings stay the same.
