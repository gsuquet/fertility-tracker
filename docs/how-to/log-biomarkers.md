# How-To: Log Biomarker Observations

This guide provides instructions for logging daily biomarker observations, entering CrMS short codes, and applying manual Peak Day overrides in **Fertility Tracker**.

---

## Logging a Standard Observation

1. Click on the target cycle day in the **Chart Row** view or open the **Observation Drawer**.
2. **Select Bleeding (if present):**
   * Heavy (`H`), Moderate (`M`), Light (`L`), Very Light (`VL`), or Brown (`B`).
   * *Note: When bleeding is selected, non-bleeding mucus options are muted unless spotting occurs.*
3. **Select Mucus Stretch:**
   * No stretch (`0`, `2`, `2W`), moderate stretch (`4`, `6`, `8`), or maximum stretch (`10`, `10DL`, `10SL`, `10WL`).
4. **Select Mucus Modifiers:**
   * Select one or more color and consistency modifiers: `B` (Brown), `C` (Cloudy), `C/K` (Cloudy/Clear), `G` (Gummy), `K` (Clear), `L` (Lubricative), `P` (Pasty), `Y` (Yellow).
5. **Select Frequency:**
   * `X1`, `X2`, `X3` (times observed per day), or `AD` (All Day).
6. **Toggle Intercourse (`I`):**
   * Enable the intercourse toggle if intercourse occurred on this cycle day.

---

## Overriding the Peak Day Manually

While **Fertility Tracker** automatically calculates Peak Day based on the last day of Peak-type mucus (clear, stretchable $\ge 10$, or lubricative), you can manually set or override the Peak Day:

1. Open the observation for the intended Peak Day in the **Observation Drawer**.
2. Toggle the **"Manual Peak Day Override"** switch to `ON`.
3. Click **Save Observation**.
4. The system will mark this day as **$P$** and automatically recalculate subsequent days as $P+1$, $P+2$, and $P+3$.

---

## Editing or Deleting an Observation

1. Click on any existing observation card in the **Chart View** or **Calendar Grid**.
2. Update the fields as desired and click **Save Changes**.
3. To remove an entry, click **Delete Observation** at the bottom of the drawer.
