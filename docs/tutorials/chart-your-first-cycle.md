# Chart your first cycle

In this tutorial, you record five days of observations. Then you look at the stamps, the Peak Day and the statistics that the application calculates.

You need about 10 minutes. You do not need to know the Creighton Model codes. This tutorial gives you each code.

## Before you start

- Open the application in a browser. Use the [public site](https://fertility-tracker.gsuquet.com) or a [local copy](../how-to/install-and-run-locally.md).
- Use a browser that has no Fertility Tracker data. A private browser window is a good choice.

## Open the application with no data

1. Open the application. The welcome guide opens.
2. Click **Next** three times.
3. Click **Start Fresh**. The **Today** page shows.

The **Today** page shows one date at a time. The date bar at the top shows the selected date.

## Record the first day of bleeding

You start four days before today.

1. Click **Previous Day** four times.
2. In **Entry Mode**, click **Direct Code Only**.
3. In **Direct Code Entry**, type `M`.
4. Click **Save Observation**. The **New Cycle Start?** dialog opens.
5. Click **Yes, Start New Cycle**.

The code `M` is moderate bleeding. The stamp preview shows a red stamp. This day is day 1 of your first cycle.

## Record a day of fertile mucus

1. Click **Next Day**.
2. In **Direct Code Entry**, type `10KLAD`.
3. Click **Save Observation**.

The code `10KLAD` is very stretchy (`10`), clear (`K`) and lubricative (`L`) mucus that you saw all day (`AD`). The stamp preview shows a white stamp with a baby. This is a fertile day.

## Record three dry days

1. Click **Next Day**.
2. In **Direct Code Entry**, type `0AD`.
3. Click **Save Observation**.
4. Do steps 1 to 3 again two times. Stop when you get to today.

The code `0AD` is dry all day. A dry day usually gets a green stamp. But these three days come after a fertile day. Thus, they get light green stamps with a baby and the numbers 1, 2 and 3.

## Look at the paper chart

1. Click **Paper Chart Strip**. On a phone, click **Chart**.
2. Find the row of your cycle.

The row shows five stamps: red, white, then three light green stamps. The white day has the letter **P**. The application found the Peak Day because it is the last day of fertile mucus.

## Look at the statistics

1. Click **Cycle Analytics**. On a phone, click **Analytics**.
2. Find **Cycle Phases Breakdown**.

The follicular phase is 2 days: day 1 to the Peak Day. The luteal phase is 3 days: the days after the Peak Day.

## What you did

You recorded a cycle with bleeding, fertile mucus and dry days. You saw the stamp of each day, the Peak Day and the count of three after the Peak Day.
